Methodology

This report analyzes slice efficiency metrics from RSLAQ NS-O-RAN-Gym experiments:

  • Algorithms: SAC Paper, SAC Resource Efficient, DDQN Paper
  • Slices: eMBB, URLLC, MTC
  • Scenarios: low_traffic, normal, congestion, stressed, insufficient_resources
  • Seeds: 5 independent runs per scenario

Metrics Explained

  • Throughput: Average data rate per slice in Mbps
  • PDR (Packet Delivery Ratio): Percentage of successfully delivered packets
  • PLR (Packet Loss Ratio): Percentage of lost packets
  • Resource Share: Percentage of total resources allocated to the slice
  • Outage Rate: Percentage of time the slice experiences service disruption
